## Record your first automation

The updated Getting Started guide opens with a short recorder video and a hands-on exercise. You can follow along on the practice page in desktop Chrome.

1. Open the practice page and select **Recording** in the extension’s Side Panel.
2. Select **Start Recording**, fill in a practice field, and click the test counter.
3. Select **Stop Recording**. Reload the practice page, then run your saved automation from **Automations** in the Side Panel if it hasn’t already run. Watch for the field to fill and the counter to increase.

On builds with the updated recorder, **Start Recording** creates a new automation. To add actions to an existing one, choose **Continue Recording** beside it in the Automations list. The guide also links to manual setup for when you want to configure a target or step directly. [Getting Started](https://getautoclicker.com/docs/5.x/getting-started/introduction/)

## Find the extension and compare plans

The homepage’s **Install extension** button opens the main Chrome Web Store listing. The download guide also lists the preview builds. [Homepage](https://getautoclicker.com/) · [Download guide](https://getautoclicker.com/docs/5.x/getting-started/download/)

**Pricing** is now linked from the main navigation. The comparison shows Free, Plus, and Pro side by side, with monthly and annual prices for paid plans. Ask AI in the Side Panel is available with sign-in. The OpenAI action you add to an automation step requires Pro. [Plans and pricing](https://getautoclicker.com/docs/5.x/about/upgrade-plan/) · [Ask AI guide](https://getautoclicker.com/docs/5.x/side-panel/ask-ai/)
