## New in This Update

Plus and Pro subscribers can now pay yearly instead of monthly — two months free compared to the monthly rate. This update also fixes Google Sign-In on Microsoft Edge and other non-Chrome browsers.

## Highlights

### 1) Yearly Billing for Plus and Pro

The Subscription page now has a **Monthly / Yearly** toggle above the plan cards. Switch to Yearly and the price on each card updates to the annual rate — Plus at **$49.90/yr** and Pro at **$99.90/yr**, both working out to two months free versus paying monthly.

If you already have an active subscription, the toggle opens set to whichever cadence you're actually on, and your current plan is only highlighted as "Current Plan" when the cadence matches too — so switching the toggle to compare doesn't make it look like you're on a plan you're not. Changing cadence still goes through the existing cancel-first flow: cancel your current subscription, then subscribe again at the cadence you want.

### 2) Google Sign-In Fixed on Edge

Signing in with Google could fail on Microsoft Edge and some other non-Chrome browsers, always showing a generic "Token not found" error. The sign-in flow now reads the redirect correctly regardless of how Google orders its response, so Google Sign-In works the same everywhere. If sign-in ever does fail, you'll now see Google's actual error message instead of the generic one.

## Why This Matters

Annual billing means one payment a year instead of remembering a monthly one — and it's cheaper. If you're already paying monthly and want to switch, cancel your current subscription first, then resubscribe with **Yearly** selected on the toggle.

See the [Upgrade Plan docs](https://getautoclicker.com/docs/5.x/about/upgrade-plan/) for full pricing details.
